# Overdrive: research for the player's follow-up

Date: 2026-09-13. Starting candidate: `c4fa60b`. This research responds to the player's reported problems: missing Quit, an unsatisfying model and laser, weak hit/death feedback, insufficiently distinct weapon upgrades, and finite predetermined levels. It does not replace the historical Skybound review.

The previous implementation's correctness and procedural mesh detail did not establish that the player enjoyed its presentation. Bungie's sandbox team explicitly acknowledged that weapon systems can behave as designed while the shooting experience remains frustrating. Treat this player's feedback as new acceptance evidence, and review the resulting experience again. [Bungie, Notes on the Hand Cannon](https://www.bungie.net/en/Forums/Post/212825953).

## Four implementation decisions

1. **Make weapons change decisions and have identifiable audiovisual signatures.** Replace the thin generic auto-shot with at least three roles: fast focused bolts, a short spread for close groups, and a slower piercing or chained attack. Give each a recognizable source, travel shape, impact and sound envelope. Upgrade cards must explain the behavioral difference and the currently equipped weapon; the next fight should demonstrate it without requiring the player to inspect a damage number.
2. **Show contact and destruction as separate events.** A hit needs a localized spark, brief silhouette-preserving flash and directional mechanical recoil. A kill needs a visible break/release followed by short-lived fragments or energy dissipation. Reserve the strongest camera/audio response for important kills or charged attacks. Bound debris and overlapping flashes; movement and danger warnings must remain readable during a kill streak.
3. **Generate varied encounters within validated spatial rules.** Use a reproducible seed to select and transform authored route, shutter and encounter modules. Vary approaches, objective order, threat combinations and hazard rhythm. Preserve safe entry space, reachable objectives/exits and recovery between pressure peaks. Milestone bosses should lead to another generated level and a meaningful reward. Continuous play needs bounded difficulty and cleanup, not only ever-growing health and enemy counts.
4. **Make leaving the game an ordinary action.** Provide an immediately visible Quit on title and pause, with a clear route back to title or an end-of-run result from checkpoints. Keep the completed run's record understandable. A test that finds a button is insufficient: activate the actual callback in an isolated process and confirm normal exit.

These decisions are local design applications of the sources below, not claims that those sources prescribe Pulsebreak's exact weapon count or timings.

## Concrete evidence to collect

- **Weapon identity:** one common test encounter for each weapon, then an upgraded encounter. Capture actual attack motion, muzzle-to-contact timing and the live mix. Verify range, spread/pierce/chain behavior, blockers, cooldowns and boss targets separately from appearance.
- **Impact/model:** normal-camera idle, running, takeoff, flight, landing, attack, nonlethal hit and death motion. Close-ups supplement these; they cannot establish the gameplay-scale result. Compare Low Effects and crowded combat so increased spectacle does not obscure danger.
- **Generation:** repeat identical seed/level pairs and compare generated structures; vary seeds and levels to establish real spatial/encounter differences; test entry/relay/exit clearances and reachable routes. Keep an independent cosmetic random stream so particle changes cannot silently rewrite level generation.
- **Continuity:** repeatedly cross genuine completion/travel states through multiple boss milestones; preserve equipment and run statistics, clear transient objects, and keep stage indexing unbounded. Retain deaths and failures. A fixture that teleports to a later stage does not demonstrate continuous progression.
- **Player acceptance:** ask the original player to compare model, firing, impact and upgrade identity in play; include uncoached attempts by new players and listening in a busy encounter. Automation establishes mechanics and stability, not enjoyment or audio quality.

The local review rubric and resulting evidence belong in [the Overdrive review](../qa/overdrive-review.md). A score above nine remains an outcome to justify with that evidence, not an implementation requirement that forces a favorable rating.
