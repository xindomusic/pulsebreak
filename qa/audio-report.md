## Shutdown leak regression fix — 2026-09-07

Reproduced `--headless --verbose --path . --quit-after 2`: two `AudioStreamWAV` and two `AudioStreamPlaybackWAV` instances leaked, each with reference count 1. Stopping players and clearing their stream references in `_exit_tree()` alone reproduced the same four leaks.

Godot's audio server queues a stop for deletion on the following audio mix; an immediate engine quit can finish the driver first. This matches the existing [Godot audio shutdown issue](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/76745) and the stop/mix lifecycle in [Godot's audio-server source](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/master/servers/audio/audio_server.cpp). The controller now stops every player, detaches the streams, allows the audio thread to drain for the next-mix interval plus 40 ms (bounded to 40–100 ms), then clears its caches. The delay happens only when the audio controller leaves the scene tree. No warnings are disabled and audio remains enabled during headless tests.

Five final full-project headless runs all exited 0 with **zero warnings, errors or leaked-instance messages**:
