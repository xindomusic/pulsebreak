05 · Core loop — steal fire, spend fire

Dash. Absorb. Break.

Weapons auto-fire — there is no aim button to learn. Skill is movement: where you dash, when you release, what you risk for one more charge.

01

DASH THROUGH FIRE

Shift / Space-tap through orange tracers to absorb them. Each shot becomes charge — 12 gold sparks + 120ms core flare.

02

RELEASE THE PULSE

Space hold-release. Ring scales with stored charge to Ø11m in 450ms ease-out cubic · 6px shake / 200ms · deck flare +150% / 300ms.

03

GLIDE · COLLECT · PUSH ON

Ctrl / A to jump, hold to glide over danger. Bank gold crystals, install upgrades at the checkpoint, push to the next sector — or bank the score.
