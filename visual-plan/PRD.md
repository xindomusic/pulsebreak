# Pulsebreak — AAA Realistic-3D Redesign: visual PRD

## 1. Goal and visual pillars

Pulsebreak is a Mac arena roguelite: a winged courier mech dashes through orange enemy shots to steal their energy, then spends that charge on a radial pulse blast. The current look reads flat and stylized. This PRD defines a completely new visual direction targeting 9/10: realistic 3D with AAA color grading and material quality. The deliverable for this round is one self-contained HTML design-approval page (no game code changes) that presents the new direction at full fidelity — hero key-art framing, in-game combat framing, palette, materials, lighting and camera — so the direction can be approved or rejected in one viewing. The two reference images are the quality bar: a render that does not read as the same world as `refs/hero.png` and `refs/combat.png` fails.

Pillars:
- Realistic PBR hard-surface: painted ceramic-white armor over dark gunmetal frame, brass-gold trim, panel lines, edge wear, grime in recesses, wet reflective deck. No flat colors, no cel shading, no toy/chibi proportions.
- AAA teal-and-orange grade: deep teal storm clouds and blue-black steel against molten orange emissives and a warm sunset key in the hero framing. Every emissive (core, tracers, deck circuits, crystals) is orange/gold; hero shadows fall to teal (see the shadow band in §3).
- Cinematic atmosphere: volumetric-feeling cloud sea below the arena, god rays from a low sun, drifting smoke, rain-wet specular streaks, floating ember/spark particles. The arena floats above clouds — no ground plane, no skybox gradient alone.
- Readability at gameplay distance: player is the only white/gold object; enemies are the only rust-red mechs (rust-brown crates exist in combat.png — §4.3 — but are inert boxes with no visor, gun arms or legs, so rust-red *mech* coding survives); danger is the only saturated translucent red; pickups are the only gold diamonds. Color coding survives the grade.

## 3. Global look

- Style: realistic PBR 3D, AAA game key-art finish. Micro-detail everywhere: panel seams 2–4 px at 1920 wide, bolt heads, stenciled markings, chipped paint on edges, oil streaks under joints, wet speculars. No texture-less unlit surfaces anywhere in frame.
- Palette (base colors, sampled from references; lit reads brighter than base):
  - Player armor ceramic white base albedo `#E8E6DE` (metallic 0.1, roughness 0.35). Under the hero teal fill, lit white plates read in the `#A8B2AE`–`#D8DEDA` band (lit chest patch median `#A4ACA9`) and shadowed white plates read in the `#5F828A`–`#7E9AA0` teal band (shadowed shoulder patch median `#64878D`).
  - Player frame / joints / gun gunmetal `#24221C`–`#2E2E30` (metallic 0.85, roughness 0.45)
  - Brass-gold trim `#DCAC70` lit sample, base `#B08A45` (metallic 1.0, roughness 0.3)
  - Chest core: gunmetal hex bezel + orange ring `#FF8A1A` + segmented radial sunburst/gear of 10–12 orange spokes inside the ring, around a near-white centre `#FDFDF1` (emissive strength 4–6, blooms on the white centre; see §4.1). Eyes and gun vents emissive orange `#FF8A1A`.
  - Deck steel blue-black `#111A21`–`#1E2A34` (metallic 0.7, roughness 0.25 wet, clearcoat-feel streak reflections)
  - Deck circuit glow emissive `#FF9A2A`; tracer rear flame `#E58B38` (slug body is dark copper, §4.4)
  - Enemy rust red base `#9C4028` (lit sample `#713D35` in shade, highlights to `#C05A3A`; metallic 0.4, roughness 0.6, heavy edge wear)
  - Crystal pickups emissive gold `#FFD75E`, core sample `#FFFBE1` (emissive strength 3)
  - Pulse ring near-white `#F2F7FA` (edge sample `#D0D8DD`, emissive strength 5 at rim)
  - Danger zone translucent crimson `#E5384A` at 30–40% opacity, hex grid lines `#FF5A6A`
  - Sky sunset warm `#F6DDBF` at sun, storm cloud teal `#2B585F`, deep cloud shadow `#03181F`, cloud-sea highlight `#7FA8B0`
  - Iridescent-puddle exception (combat.png only): ≥6 oil-rainbow puddle patches, each 0.5–1.5 m across, with low-saturation green/magenta/cyan sheen over the dark deck (e.g. at ~(1180, 260) and ~(1420, 560) in the 1920×1280 combat frame). This sheen is the only permitted green/magenta/cyan in either framing; it is a thin-film reflection, never an emissive or a flat fill.
- Lighting — hero framing (hero.png): one warm key — low sun from frame-left, ~15° above horizon, color `#FFD9A8`, rim-lighting the mech's left edges. No long frame-right mech shadow is visible on the plinth in hero.png — the mech shadow falls behind/off the plinth; the only contact darkening on the plinth top is the broad dark contact/reflection patch under the feet (~x 1150–1650, y 1000–1150 in the 1920×1280 hero frame). Cool ambient/hemisphere fill from teal sky `#2B585F` at ~35% of key intensity so shadows read in the teal band above. Orange point/area accents from deck glow, core and foundry windows (small, numerous, never brighter than the key). Sun glow centre at ~(15%, 11%) of frame with god rays fanning to ~(35%, 30%).
- Lighting — combat framing (combat.png): bright high daylight, a separate rig from the hero. High key at ~60° elevation from frame-top-left, ambient/hemisphere fill at ~70% of key intensity, short shadows under enemies (shadow length ≤0.3× object height), sky-blue deck reflections, no sunset rims and no long frame-right shadows.
- Both framings: ACES-filmic tone mapping, exposure set so armor whites hold detail (no clipped white except emissive cores and the pulse rim). Contact: the hero deck gives a planar wet mirror reflection of the shins plus the broad dark contact/reflection patch under the feet (~x 1150–1650, y 1000–1150) and a small toe contact shadow under each contacting toe (see §4.1 feet); no floating, and no long cast mech shadow on the plinth top.
- Hero weather (hero.png only): diagonal rain streaks at ~75° from horizontal (steep, wind-blown toward frame-right) fill the upper-left quadrant *and* the top-center sky over the foundry (~x 450–1100, y 0–~320) — i.e. the upper ~55% of frame width — density high enough to read as streak texture at 1920 wide, and near-zero over the mech silhouette (x >1250); ≥100 ember/spark dots (1–3 px) spread over the whole plinth top (~x 600–1900, y ~850–1280), not clustered around the legs — counted by the plinth-area sample in AC-09; sun glow and god rays as above.
- Camera (hero framing, hero.png): three-quarter front-left of the mech at ~waist-to-chest height with a slight down-tilt onto the decks (the arena-disc top surface and the foreground deck top are both clearly visible while the mech towers in frame). No lens "feel" criterion: background compression is checked by the measurable proxy that the §4.3 fan wheel renders ~220 px wide (bbox x ~1170–1390) against mech H ≈ 1025 px, i.e. fan-wheel width / H ≈ 0.21 (±0.03), with the arena disc centre at (47%, 35%). Measurable framing: mech occupies x 52–94% and y 11–93% of frame; arena disc center at (47%, 35%); horizon at y ≈ 16%.
- Camera (gameplay framing, combat.png): oblique top-down, ~52° from vertical (deck strongly foreshortened, mech heads/shoulders readable), FOV 45°, deck long axis at −30° in frame (deck long edges run ~30° below horizontal, TL→BR), no horizon in frame except cloud gaps at deck edges. Player at frame center (47%, 42%); visible deck ≈ 26 m × 17 m at the scale in §4. Checkable tilt proxy: the named square deck grate at bbox ~x 1470–1660, y 700–820 renders at ~190×120 px (axis ratio ~0.63) in the 1920×1280 combat frame (second square grate, bottom-centre ~x 960–1180, y 1080–1230, ~220×150 px, ratio ~0.68, as corroboration; proxy band 0.63–0.68).
- Background / ground: no ground. Below and beyond the deck is cloud sea filling ≥40% of hero frame by the segmentation rule in AC-09 (lower-left mass), layered cumulus with teal shadow cores and warm sunlit tops, plus foundry silhouettes in haze at hero.png upper third. Gameplay frame edges show cloud void on at least 2 sides.
- Render: HTML approval page at 1920×1280 reference aspect (3:2) panels; any live 3D viewport uses PBR + ACES + bloom; static panels must be indistinguishable in grade from the references. sRGB output.
