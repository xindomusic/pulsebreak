05 · Core loop — steal fire, spend fire

Dash. Absorb. Break.

Weapons auto-fire — there is no aim button to learn. Skill is movement: where you dash, when you release, what you risk for one more charge.

01

DASH THROUGH FIRE

Space dash (toward movement) through orange tracers to absorb them. Each shot becomes charge — 12 gold sparks + 120ms core flare.

02

RELEASE THE PULSE

E to release. Ring scales with stored charge to Ø11m in 450ms ease-out cubic · 6px shake / 200ms · deck flare +150% / 300ms.

03

GLIDE · COLLECT · PUSH ON

F to jump, hold F to glide over danger. Bank gold crystals, install upgrades at the checkpoint, push to the next sector — or bank the score.
